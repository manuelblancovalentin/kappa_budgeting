# 💡 Decisions
<PageMeta />
---

<TBox type="summary" title="Purpose">

This section records decisions that should survive beyond a single notebook, meeting, or experiment. Use it for design choices, naming conventions, architectural pivots, and decisions that future collaborators should not have to rediscover.

</TBox>

## Current Pages

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [ADR Template](./adr-template.md) | Template for recording a new architecture or research decision. |

## When To Add A Decision

Add a decision record when:

- a choice affects future experiments or documentation structure,
- a method was rejected and we should remember why,
- a naming convention or ID scheme becomes stable,
- a tradeoff is important enough that a future collaborator may ask about it.

## Suggested Format

Use short ADR-style entries:

```text
Context
Decision
Consequences
Alternatives considered
Status
```
